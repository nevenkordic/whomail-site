Everything a mail app owes you

  1. One inbox for every account

    Outlook and Microsoft 365, iCloud, Fastmail, Yahoo and any IMAP server, together in a unified inbox or kept apart — your choice. Full folder support, move to folder, drafts that send from the reading bar.

  2. Instant push, without the creep

    New-mail banners land in seconds while the app is open and within about a minute when it's closed — included for free. The push itself carries no mail content — the rich preview is fetched by your device, from your mailbox, after the banner arrives. Mark read, delete or reply straight from the notification.

  3. Rules, snooze and send later

    File newsletters automatically with mail rules. Snooze a message until you can deal with it, or write now and schedule the send — snooze and Send Later are Pro.

  4. Insights — who you actually mail

    On-device analytics: volume, reply time, a week heatmap, and ranked people. Open someone for timeline, files, network and notes. No cloud AI. The full ranked list is Pro; the top two are free.

  5. Calendar and invitations built in

    Meeting invites show an Accept / Tentative / Decline banner right on the message. Tap an .ics attachment to add the event to your calendar. Month view, events, tasks, holidays — covered.

  6. Search that actually finds things

    Full-text search across subjects, senders, recipients and the entire body of every message — plus operators such as from: and has:attachment — and it reaches into the Outlook server for mail you haven't synced yet.

  7. Native on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android

    Built as a native app, not a web page in a window. It launches fast, scrolls smooth, respects dark mode and keeps working offline.

  8. Readable email, even the badly built kind

    Every message renders on a clean paper card that follows light or dark theme, so newsletters can't turn their own text invisible or paint opaque bars. Remote images stay blocked until you say so.

Subject: What we can't see

Private by architecture, not by promise

Your mail is processed on your device and stored in your device's keychain-protected database. Notification relays never see message content — pushes say only "new mail", and your device fills in the rest itself. Remove an account and any stored credential is deleted from our relay in the same breath.

There is no cloud AI, no analytics profile, no ad model waiting in the wings. WhoMail is paid software with one customer: you.
