## Where contacts come from

WhoMail builds a contacts list from the mail you have synced —
you do not import an address book. Open the **Contacts** tab to
browse everyone WhoMail has seen.

Each row can show relationship strength, last contact, and an
overdue-reply hint. Tap a row for the person profile.

Contacts themselves are free. The full Insights ranking and the
seven-chip Insights profile are [WhoMail Pro](../pro/). From
Contacts, a profile opens on **Info** and **Notes**.

## Filter and sort

- **Search** at the top filters by name or address.
- **People / Services** — campaign and no-reply senders are
  Services so they don't own the A–Z list. The chip still finds
  them.
- **Tag chips** — tap a tag you have assigned to narrow the list.
- **Sort** (toolbar) — name, strength, or last contact. Optionally
  **group by company** (from the email domain).

## The contact profile

The header starts expanded. **Show details** / **Hide details**
toggles the extra chrome.

- **Info** — name, company, phones, websites and social links
  extracted from signatures (see
  [People Lens](../insights/#people-lens)).
- **Notes** — yours only, stored on this device.

From Insights, the same person has Timeline, Conversations, Files,
Network and Links as well.

## Photos

Where WhoMail has a photo for a sender (provider photo or a
cached avatar), it shows on the inbox row and the profile. Photos
are stored on the device with the rest of the mail cache.
