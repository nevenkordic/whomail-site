## The unified inbox

**All Inboxes** shows new mail from every account in one list. Tap an
account in the sidebar (iPad and Mac) or the account list (iPhone) to
see a single account's folders instead.

Unread messages are marked with a coral dot. The app badge counts unread
inbox mail across your active accounts.

## Reading a message

Every email renders on a light paper card — even in dark mode. This is
deliberate: many newsletters ship broken dark-mode styles that turn
their own text invisible, and the paper card keeps everything readable.

Links open in your browser. Attachments preview in place; tap to save
or share them.

## Move to folder

Open a message and use the folder button to move it to any folder in
the account. On Mac you'll find it in the action bar; on iPhone it's in
the action pill at the bottom of the message.

## Snooze

Not ready to deal with a message? Long-press it in the message list (or
right-click on Mac) and choose **Snooze**. Snoozed mail disappears from
the inbox and the badge until the time you picked, then returns with a
reminder notification.

Find everything you've snoozed in the **Snoozed** view — sidebar on
iPad and Mac, the **More** tab on iPhone. You can unsnooze anything
early from there.

## Deleting

Deleted messages move to the account's Trash folder — they aren't
permanently erased until the server clears them or you empty Trash.

## Drafts

Drafts save automatically and sync to your account's Drafts folder, so
a message started on your iPhone can be finished on your Mac. Scheduled
drafts live in the [Outbox](../compose/#send-later) instead.
