## Composing

Tap the compose button anywhere to start a new message. The composer
supports rich text — bold, italics, lists, links — plus Cc and Bcc,
and quotes the original message when you reply or forward.

## Signatures

Set a signature in **Settings → Signature**. Rich formatting is
supported, and the signature is appended automatically to new messages
and replies.

## Attachments

Attach files with the paperclip in the compose toolbar. On Mac you can
also drag files from Finder straight into the compose window.

## Send later

The **Send** button is also a menu: tap it to send now, or hold it
(click-and-hold on Mac) to choose **Send Later** with a preset time —
this evening, tomorrow morning, next week.

Scheduled messages wait in the **Outbox** (sidebar on iPad and Mac,
More tab on iPhone), where you can edit them, send immediately, or
cancel the schedule.

> Scheduling happens on your device, not on a server — WhoMail never
> uploads your drafts anywhere. The trade-off: a scheduled message is
> sent the next time the app is open at or after the scheduled time.

Send Later currently works with Outlook accounts, for messages that
have no attachments.

## Automatic replies (out of office)

For Outlook and Microsoft 365 accounts, set automatic replies in
**Settings → Automatic Replies** — with separate messages for people
inside and outside your organisation, and an optional time window.
