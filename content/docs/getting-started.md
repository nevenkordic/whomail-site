## Install WhoMail

WhoMail runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Install it from the App Store on
each device you use — your accounts are set up per device, and your
credentials never leave your device's keychain.

## Add your first account

1. Open WhoMail and tap **Add Account** (on first launch the welcome
   screen takes you straight there).
2. Choose your provider and sign in.

Your first account is free. Adding a second account requires
[WhoMail Pro](../pro/).

### Outlook and Microsoft 365

Choose **Outlook**, then sign in with your Microsoft account in the
sign-in sheet that appears. WhoMail uses Microsoft's own sign-in — your
password is never typed into WhoMail itself. Work and school accounts,
Outlook.com and Hotmail addresses all work.

Outlook accounts get the deepest integration: server-side search,
calendar sync, meeting invite responses and automatic replies
(out-of-office).

### Gmail

Choose **Gmail** and sign in with Google. As with Microsoft, sign-in
happens on Google's own sheet.

### iCloud

Choose **iCloud**. You'll need an **app-specific password** rather than
your Apple ID password:

1. Go to [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com) → Sign-In and
   Security → App-Specific Passwords.
2. Create one named "WhoMail" and paste it into WhoMail.

### Fastmail and other IMAP providers

Choose **IMAP** and enter your email address and password. For Fastmail,
create an app password first (Fastmail Settings → Privacy & Security →
Integrations). WhoMail fills in the server details for well-known
providers; for anything else you can enter the IMAP and SMTP servers
manually.

## Choose how much mail to sync

By default WhoMail syncs your recent mail history to your device so
search and reading work offline. You can change the sync window per
account in **Settings → Accounts**.
