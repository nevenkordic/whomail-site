## The Calendar tab

WhoMail includes a calendar with month, week and day views (month is
the default). Outlook and Microsoft 365 calendars sync automatically;
other accounts get a local calendar on the device.

## Responding to meeting invitations

When a meeting invitation lands in an Outlook inbox, the message shows
an invitation banner with **Accept**, **Tentative** and **Decline**
buttons. Your response is sent to the organiser and the event appears
in (or disappears from) your calendar straight away.

Invitations arriving in IMAP accounts (for example a Google Calendar
invite sent to a Fastmail address) are recognised and shown with an
invitation banner too.

## Adding .ics files to your calendar

Tap an `.ics` attachment and choose:

- **Add to WhoMail Calendar** — the event is parsed on-device and filed
  into your synced Outlook calendar, or the local calendar otherwise.
  It shows up in the Calendar tab immediately.
- **Open in Calendar…** — hands the file to Apple Calendar instead.

All-day events, time zones and recurring basics are handled; the event
appears with its title, time and location.
